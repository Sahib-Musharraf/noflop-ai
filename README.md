# Noflop.ai 🚨
### Don't let your idea flop.

Noflop.ai is an AI-powered idea validation tool 
for builders, vibe coders, and first-time founders. 
Paste your idea. Get a brutal, honest signal in 
30 seconds — before you write a single line of code.

---

## The Problem

42% of startups fail because they build something 
nobody wants. Not bad code. Bad ideas that were 
never validated.

Vibe coding made building easy.
It made building the wrong thing easier too.

---

## What Noflop.ai Does

You paste your idea. The AI runs it through:

- **Signal** — Build it. Kill it. Pivot it.
- **Score** — Honest rating out of 10
- **Brutal Truth** — Market demand, timing, competition
- **User Research** — Exact questions to ask 
  real users before building

No glaze. No hype. Just signal.
